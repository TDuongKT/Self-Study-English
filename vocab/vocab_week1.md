## 📝 English-English-Vietnamese Vocabulary List:

*   **Spectacular**  (adj): Very impressive to look at; breathtaking *(Hùng vĩ, ngoạn mục)*
*   **Isolated**  (adj): Far away from other places, buildings, or people; remote *(Biệt lập, cô lập, hẻo lánh)*
*   **Severe**  (adj): Very bad, serious, or unpleasant (often used for weather, illness, or punishment) *(Nghiêm trọng, khốc liệt, dữ dội)*
*   **Inevitable**  (adj): Certain to happen and unable to be avoided or prevented *(Không thể tránh khỏi)*
*   **Plunge**  (v): To fall quickly and suddenly from a high position *(Lao xuống, lao vào)*
*   **Debris**  (n): Broken or torn pieces of something larger (like buildings, rocks, or vehicles after an accident) *(Mảnh vỡ, đống đổ nát, mảnh vụn)*
*   **Acute**  (adj): (Of a bad situation, pain, or illness) very severe, extreme, or happening quickly *(Cấp tính, dữ dội, nhạy bén)*
*   **Fragile**  (adj): Easily broken, damaged, or destroyed; in a weak state *(Dễ vỡ, mỏng manh)*
*   **Tragic**  (adj): Very sad, often involving death, suffering, or extreme destruction *(Bi thảm, thảm thương)*
*   **Coverage**  (n): The reporting of a particular important event or subject in newspapers, on television, or on the internet *(Sự đưa tin, tường thuật, độ bao phủ)*
*   **Elaborate**  (adj): Containing a lot of careful detail or many detailed parts; complicated *(Tỉ mỉ, công phu, chau chuốt)*
*   **Controversial**  (adj): Causing a lot of disagreement, argument, or discussion *(Gây tranh cãi)*
*   **Distinctive**  (adj): Something that is easy to recognize because it is different from other things *(Đặc biệt, mang tính khác biệt)*
*   **Authorities**  (n): The group of people with official legal power to make decisions, give orders, or enforce laws (like the police or government) *(Chính quyền, nhà chức trách)*
*   **Curriculum**  (n): The complete set of subjects or courses studied in a school, college, etc *(Chương trình giảng dạy)*
*   **Compulsory**  (adj): If something is compulsory, you must do it because of a rule or law *(Bắt buộc)*
*   **Accumulate**  (v): To collect or gather a large amount of things (like knowledge or money) over a period of time *(Tích lũy, tích tụ)*
*   **Struggle**  (v): To experience difficulty and make a very great effort in order to do something *(Vật lộn, đấu tranh, nỗ lực)*
*   **Interact**  (v): To communicate with or react to other people *(Tương tác)*
*   **Peers**  (n): People who are the same age as you, or who have the same social position or abilities *(Người ngang hàng, bạn cùng lứa)*
*   **Fascinating**  (adj): Extremely interesting and holding your attention completely *(Cực kỳ thú vị, lôi cuốn)*
*   **Passion**  (n): A very powerful feeling of love, enthusiasm, or strong interest in doing something *(Đam mê, nhiệt huyết)*
*   **Obstacle**  (n): Something that blocks your way so that movement, going forward, or action is prevented or made more difficult *(Trở ngại, vật chướng ngại)*
*   **Thoroughly**  (adv): Completely, very much, or with great attention to every detail *(Một cách hoàn toàn, kỹ lưỡng)*
*   **Accomplish**  (v): To finish something successfully or to achieve a specific goal *(Hoàn thành, đạt được)*
*   **Prestigious**  (adj): Very much respected and admired, usually because of being important or of high quality *(Có uy tín, có thanh thế)*
*   **Beneficial**  (adj): Helpful, useful, or having a good effect *(Có lợi, ích lợi)*
*   **Ultimately**  (adv): Finally, after a series of things have happened; in the end *(Cuối cùng, rốt cuộc)*
*   **Motorist**  (n): A person who drives a car *(Người lái xe hơi)*
*   **Accidentally**  (adv): By chance or by mistake; without intending to *(Một cách tình cờ, vô tình)*
*   **Collision**  (n): An accident that happens when two vehicles hit each other with force *(Vụ va chạm, sự va chạm)*
*   **Slam**  (v): To crash into something with a lot of force; to shut forcefully *(Tông sầm, đóng sầm)*
*   **Crush**  (v): To press something very hard so that it is broken or its shape is destroyed *(Nghiền nát, đè bẹp)*
*   **Evident**  (adj): Easily seen or understood; obvious *(Hiển nhiên, rõ rệt)*
*   **Fragment**  (n): A small piece or a part, especially when something has broken *(Mảnh vỡ, mảnh, mẩu)*
*   **Evacuate**  (v): To move people from a dangerous place to somewhere safe *(Sơ tán)*
*   **Minimal**  (adj): Very small in amount or degree *(Tối thiểu)*
*   **Identification**  (n): The act of recognizing and naming someone or something (or finding the cause of something) *(Sự nhận dạng, nhận diện)*
*   **Workout**  (n): A period of physical exercise *(Buổi tập luyện, luyện tập thể chất)*
*   **Trauma**  (n): Severe physical injury (or severe emotional shock) *(Chấn thương, tổn thương tâm lý/thể chất)*
*   **Trigger**  (v): To cause something to start or happen quickly *(Kích hoạt, gây ra)*
*   **Undergo**  (v): To experience something that is unpleasant or something that involves a medical procedure/change *(Trải qua)*
*   **Therapist**  (n): Someone whose job is to treat a particular type of mental or physical illness or injury *(Bác sĩ trị liệu, nhà trị liệu)*
*   **Visible**  (adj): Able to be seen *(Có thể nhìn thấy được, rõ ràng)*
*   **Weaken**  (v): To lose strength, or to make somebody/something lose strength *(Yếu đi, làm yếu đi)*
*   **Tissue**  (n): A group of connected cells in an animal or plant that are similar to each other *(Mô tế bào, khăn giấy)*
*   **Vein**  (n): A tube that carries blood to the heart from the other parts of the body *(Tĩnh mạch, mạch máu)*
*   **Useless**  (adj): Of no value; not working or not achieving what is needed *(Vô dụng)*
*   **Well-being**  (n): The state of feeling healthy, happy, and comfortable *(Tình trạng sức khỏe, hạnh phúc)*
*   **Preserve**  (v): To keep something as it is, especially in order to prevent it from decaying or being damaged or destroyed *(Bảo tồn, giữ gìn)*
*   **Deforestation**  (n): The cutting down of trees in a large area, or the destruction of forests by people *(Nạn phá rừng, sự phá rừng)*
*   **Habitat**  (n): The natural environment in which an animal or plant usually lives *(Môi trường sống)*
*   **Endangered**  (adj): In danger of being harmed, lost, or unsuccessful (especially of animals) *(Bị đe dọa, nguy cấp)*
*   **Ecosystem**  (n): All the living things in an area and the way they affect each other and the environment *(Hệ sinh thái)*
*   **Drought**  (n): A long period when there is little or no rain *(Hạn hán)*
*   **Tackle**  (v): To try to deal with something or someone, especially a difficult problem *(Giải quyết, xử lý)*
*   **Renewable**  (adj): Renewable forms of energy can be produced as quickly as they are used *(Có thể tái tạo)*
*   **Emission**  (n): An amount of a substance that is produced and sent out into the air that is harmful to the environment *(Lượng khí thải, sự phát thải)*
*   **Sustainable**  (adj): Able to continue over a period of time, causing little or no damage to the environment *(Bền vững)*
*   **Entrepreneur**  (n): Someone who starts their own business, especially when this involves seeing a new opportunity *(Doanh nhân, nhà khởi nghiệp)*
*   **Bankruptcy**  (n): A situation in which a business or a person becomes bankrupt and cannot pay their debts *(Sự phá sản, tình trạng phá sản)*
*   **Strategy**  (n): A detailed plan for achieving success in situations such as business, politics, or sport *(Chiến lược)*
*   **Revenue**  (n): The income that a government or company receives regularly *(Doanh thu)*
*   **Recruit**  (v): To persuade someone to work for a company or become a new member of an organization *(Tuyển dụng)*
*   **Collaborate**  (v): To work with someone else for a special purpose *(Hợp tác)*
*   **Productivity**  (n): The rate at which a person, company, or country does useful work *(Năng suất)*
*   **Implement**  (v): To start using a plan or system *(Triển khai, thực hiện)*
*   **Negotiate**  (v): To have formal discussions with someone in order to reach an agreement with them *(Đàm phán, thương lượng)*
*   **Promote**  (v): To raise someone to a higher or more important position or rank *(Thăng chức, quảng bá, thúc đẩy)*
*   **Resign**  (v): To give up a job or position by telling your employer that you are leaving *(Từ chức)*
