## 📝 English-English-Vietnamese Vocabulary List:

*   **Stamina**  (n): The physical or mental strength that allows you to continue doing something for a long time *(Sức bền, thể lực)*
*   **Posture**  (n): The way in which someone holds their body when standing or sitting *(Tư thế, dáng đứng/ngồi)*
*   **Cardio**  (n): Exercise that makes the heart beat faster, such as running, cycling, or swimming *(Bài tập tim mạch)*
*   **Calorie**  (n): A unit used to measure the amount of energy that food provides *(Calo, đơn vị năng lượng)*
*   **Strain**  (n): An injury to a muscle caused by stretching or using it too much *(Sự căng cơ)*
*   **Sprain**  (n): An injury to a joint, such as an ankle or wrist, caused by suddenly twisting it *(Sự bong gân)*
*   **Warm-up**  (n): A set of gentle exercises done to prepare the body before more intense activity *(Bài khởi động)*
*   **Plateau**  (n): A period during which there is little or no further progress after a time of improvement *(Giai đoạn chững lại)*
*   **Repetition**  (n): One complete movement of an exercise, done again and again; the act of repeating something *(Lần lặp lại, sự lặp lại)*
*   **Supplement**  (n): Something added to your diet, such as vitamins or protein powder, to improve health *(Thực phẩm bổ sung)*
*   **Enroll**  (v): To officially register as a member of a course, school, or program *(Đăng ký (khóa học), ghi danh)*
*   **Lecture**  (n): A formal talk on a serious subject given to a group of students *(Bài giảng)*
*   **Assignment**  (n): A piece of work given to someone as part of their studies or job *(Bài tập, nhiệm vụ được giao)*
*   **Procrastinate**  (v): To keep delaying something that must be done, often because it is unpleasant or boring *(Trì hoãn, chần chừ)*
*   **Plagiarism**  (n): The act of copying someone else's work or ideas and pretending they are your own *(Sự đạo văn)*
*   **Distraction**  (n): Something that takes your attention away from what you should be doing *(Sự xao nhãng, điều gây mất tập trung)*
*   **Self-paced**  (adj): (Of a course) allowing learners to study at their own speed *(Tự điều chỉnh tốc độ học)*
*   **Certificate**  (n): An official document that proves you have completed a course or passed an exam *(Chứng chỉ)*
*   **Tuition**  (n): The money paid for being taught, especially at a school or university *(Học phí)*
*   **Submit**  (v): To give a document or piece of work to someone so that it can be considered or graded *(Nộp)*
*   **Immigrant**  (n): A person who comes to live permanently in a foreign country *(Người nhập cư)*
*   **Visa**  (n): An official document or stamp that allows you to enter, stay in, or leave a country *(Thị thực)*
*   **Residency**  (n): The legal right to live in a country for a period of time *(Quyền cư trú)*
*   **Citizenship**  (n): The legal right to belong to a particular country *(Quyền công dân, quốc tịch)*
*   **Assimilate**  (v): To become part of a new group or society by adopting its customs and way of life *(Hòa nhập, đồng hóa)*
*   **Homesick**  (adj): Feeling sad because you are far away from your home and family *(Nhớ nhà)*
*   **Culture shock**  (n): A feeling of confusion and anxiety when you experience a very different culture for the first time *(Sốc văn hóa)*
*   **Bureaucracy**  (n): A system with many complicated rules and processes, especially in government, that makes things slow *(Bộ máy quan liêu, thủ tục rườm rà)*
*   **Barrier**  (n): Something that prevents people from communicating, understanding each other, or making progress *(Rào cản)*
*   **Expatriate**  (n): A person who lives outside their own country *(Người sống ở nước ngoài, kiều dân)*
*   **Earthquake**  (n): A sudden, violent shaking of the ground caused by movements in the earth's surface *(Động đất)*
*   **Tremor**  (n): A slight shaking movement of the ground, often before or after an earthquake *(Rung chấn nhẹ)*
*   **Aftershock**  (n): A smaller earthquake that follows a larger one *(Dư chấn)*
*   **Collapse**  (v): To fall down suddenly because of weakness or damage *(Sụp đổ)*
*   **Rubble**  (n): Broken pieces of stone, brick, and concrete left after a building has been destroyed *(Đống gạch vụn, đống đổ nát)*
*   **Survivor**  (n): A person who continues to live after an accident, disaster, or illness *(Người sống sót)*
*   **Casualty**  (n): A person who is injured or killed in a serious accident or disaster *(Thương vong, nạn nhân)*
*   **Seismic**  (adj): Relating to earthquakes or vibrations of the earth *(Thuộc về địa chấn)*
*   **Rescue**  (n): The act of saving someone from a dangerous or difficult situation *(Sự cứu hộ, giải cứu)*
*   **Relief**  (n): Food, money, or other help given to people in need, especially after a disaster *(Hàng cứu trợ, sự cứu trợ)*
*   **Screenplay**  (n): The written text of a film, including the words spoken and directions for the actors *(Kịch bản phim)*
*   **Director**  (n): The person who controls how a film is made and guides the actors *(Đạo diễn)*
*   **Sequel**  (n): A film, book, or game that continues the story of an earlier one *(Phần tiếp theo)*
*   **Box office**  (n): The total amount of money a film earns from ticket sales; the place where tickets are sold *(Doanh thu phòng vé, quầy bán vé)*
*   **Plot**  (n): The series of events that form the story of a film, novel, or play *(Cốt truyện)*
*   **Cast**  (n): All the actors who perform in a film or play *(Dàn diễn viên)*
*   **Premiere**  (n): The first public showing of a film or play *(Buổi công chiếu đầu tiên)*
*   **Critic**  (n): A person whose job is to give opinions about books, films, or art *(Nhà phê bình)*
*   **Blockbuster**  (n): A film or book that is extremely popular and successful *(Phim bom tấn)*
*   **Subtitle**  (n): Words shown at the bottom of a screen that translate or record what is being said *(Phụ đề)*
*   **Experiment**  (n): A scientific test done to discover what happens or to prove an idea *(Thí nghiệm)*
*   **Laboratory**  (n): A room or building equipped for scientific research and experiments *(Phòng thí nghiệm)*
*   **Variable**  (n): A factor in an experiment that can change and affect the result *(Biến số)*
*   **Sample**  (n): A small amount of something taken for testing or examination *(Mẫu, mẫu thử)*
*   **Analyze**  (v): To examine something in detail in order to understand it *(Phân tích)*
*   **Conclusion**  (n): A judgment or decision reached after careful thought or study *(Kết luận)*
*   **Replicate**  (v): To repeat an experiment or study to see if the same results are produced *(Lặp lại, tái tạo (thí nghiệm))*
*   **Peer review**  (n): The evaluation of scientific work by other experts in the same field before it is published *(Bình duyệt, thẩm định đồng cấp)*
*   **Data**  (n): Facts or information, especially numbers, collected to be examined and used *(Dữ liệu)*
*   **Contaminate**  (v): To make something impure or dirty by adding a harmful or unwanted substance *(Làm nhiễm bẩn, làm ô nhiễm)*
