## 📝 English-English-Vietnamese Vocabulary List:

*   **Retire**  (v): To stop working, usually because you have reached a certain age *(Nghỉ hưu)*
*   **Pension**  (n): An amount of money paid regularly to someone who has stopped working because of old age *(Lương hưu)*
*   **Elderly**  (adj): (Of a person) old, or becoming old *(Cao tuổi, lớn tuổi)*
*   **Dementia**  (n): A serious illness of the brain that affects memory and the ability to think clearly *(Chứng mất trí nhớ, sa sút trí tuệ)*
*   **Caregiver**  (n): A person who regularly looks after someone who is old, sick, or disabled *(Người chăm sóc)*
*   **Nursing home**  (n): A place where old people live and are cared for when they cannot look after themselves *(Viện dưỡng lão)*
*   **Longevity**  (n): Long life, or the fact of lasting a long time *(Tuổi thọ, sự sống lâu)*
*   **Frail**  (adj): Weak and thin, especially because of old age or illness *(Yếu ớt, mỏng manh)*
*   **Independence**  (n): The ability to live and make decisions without help or control from others *(Sự độc lập, tự lập)*
*   **Nostalgic**  (adj): Feeling happy and a little sad when thinking about the past *(Hoài niệm, nhớ về quá khứ)*
*   **Esports**  (n): Competitive video gaming, especially when played professionally in front of an audience *(Thể thao điện tử)*
*   **Tournament**  (n): A competition in which many players or teams compete over a series of games *(Giải đấu)*
*   **Opponent**  (n): A person or team that you compete against in a game or contest *(Đối thủ)*
*   **Tactic**  (n): A planned action or method used to achieve a particular goal, especially in a game or contest *(Chiến thuật)*
*   **Reflex**  (n): A quick physical reaction to something, done without thinking *(Phản xạ)*
*   **Spectator**  (n): A person who watches an event, especially a sports event *(Khán giả, người xem)*
*   **Sponsor**  (n): A company or person that pays for an event or team, usually in return for advertising *(Nhà tài trợ)*
*   **Rank**  (n): A position in a list or system that shows how good or important someone is compared to others *(Thứ hạng, cấp bậc)*
*   **Teammate**  (n): A person who plays on the same team as you *(Đồng đội)*
*   **Livestream**  (n): A live video broadcast over the internet *(Phát trực tiếp)*
*   **Power outage**  (n): A period of time when there is no electricity supply *(Sự mất điện, cúp điện)*
*   **Grid**  (n): A network of cables and power stations that carries electricity across an area *(Lưới điện)*
*   **Solar panel**  (n): A flat device that converts sunlight into electricity *(Tấm pin năng lượng mặt trời)*
*   **Wind turbine**  (n): A tall machine with large blades that uses the wind to produce electricity *(Tua-bin gió)*
*   **Fossil fuel**  (n): A fuel such as coal, oil, or gas formed from the remains of ancient plants and animals *(Nhiên liệu hóa thạch)*
*   **Consumption**  (n): The amount of energy, food, or other resources that is used *(Sự tiêu thụ, lượng tiêu thụ)*
*   **Generate**  (v): To produce something, especially energy such as electricity or heat *(Tạo ra, sản xuất (năng lượng))*
*   **Shortage**  (n): A situation in which there is not enough of something that is needed *(Sự thiếu hụt)*
*   **Carbon footprint**  (n): The total amount of carbon dioxide produced by the activities of a person or organization *(Lượng khí thải carbon (dấu chân carbon))*
*   **Insulation**  (n): Material used to stop heat, cold, or sound from passing through walls or roofs *(Vật liệu cách nhiệt, sự cách nhiệt)*
*   **Acquaintance**  (n): A person you know but who is not a close friend *(Người quen)*
*   **Gossip**  (n): Talk about other people's private lives, often unkind or not true *(Chuyện ngồi lê đôi mách, tin đồn nhảm)*
*   **Rumor**  (n): A story or piece of information that may or may not be true, passed from person to person *(Tin đồn)*
*   **Betray**  (v): To be disloyal to someone who trusts you, for example by telling their secrets *(Phản bội)*
*   **Trustworthy**  (adj): Able to be trusted; honest and reliable *(Đáng tin cậy)*
*   **Socialize**  (v): To spend time with other people in a friendly way *(Giao lưu, hòa nhập xã hội)*
*   **Awkward**  (adj): Causing or feeling embarrassment; difficult to deal with *(Lúng túng, ngượng ngùng)*
*   **Rapport**  (n): A friendly relationship in which people understand each other well *(Mối quan hệ hòa hợp, sự thân thiết)*
*   **Grudge**  (n): A feeling of anger or dislike toward someone because of something they did in the past *(Mối hận, sự thù hằn)*
*   **Supportive**  (adj): Giving help, encouragement, or sympathy to someone *(Luôn ủng hộ, hỗ trợ)*
*   **Advertisement**  (n): A picture, short film, or piece of text that tries to persuade people to buy a product or service *(Quảng cáo)*
*   **Brand**  (n): A type of product made by a particular company, with its own name and identity *(Thương hiệu)*
*   **Slogan**  (n): A short, easily remembered phrase used in advertising or by a political group *(Khẩu hiệu)*
*   **Target audience**  (n): The particular group of people that an advertisement or product is aimed at *(Đối tượng khách hàng mục tiêu)*
*   **Commercial**  (n): An advertisement on television or radio *(Quảng cáo trên truyền hình/radio)*
*   **Endorse**  (v): To publicly say that you support or approve of a product, person, or idea *(Quảng bá, xác nhận ủng hộ)*
*   **Logo**  (n): A design or symbol used by a company to identify its products *(Biểu tượng, logo)*
*   **Consumer**  (n): A person who buys goods or services for their own use *(Người tiêu dùng)*
*   **Loyalty**  (n): The quality of continuing to support or buy from a person, brand, or organization *(Sự trung thành)*
*   **Launch**  (v): To introduce a new product or start a new activity *(Ra mắt, tung ra)*
*   **Novel**  (n): A long written story about imaginary people and events *(Tiểu thuyết)*
*   **Author**  (n): A person who writes books or articles *(Tác giả)*
*   **Protagonist**  (n): The main character in a story, play, or film *(Nhân vật chính)*
*   **Narrative**  (n): A story or a description of a series of events *(Câu chuyện, lối kể chuyện)*
*   **Chapter**  (n): One of the main parts into which a book is divided *(Chương (sách))*
*   **Fiction**  (n): Literature that describes imaginary people and events, not real ones *(Truyện hư cấu)*
*   **Genre**  (n): A particular type or style of literature, art, film, or music *(Thể loại)*
*   **Manuscript**  (n): The original version of a book or article before it is printed *(Bản thảo)*
*   **Publish**  (v): To prepare and print a book, magazine, etc. so that it can be sold to the public *(Xuất bản)*
*   **Bestseller**  (n): A book or other product that is bought by very large numbers of people *(Sách bán chạy nhất)*
