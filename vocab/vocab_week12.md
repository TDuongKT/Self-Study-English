## 📝 English-English-Vietnamese Vocabulary List:

*   **Mechanic**  (n): A person whose job is to repair and maintain vehicles or machines *(Thợ sửa xe, thợ máy)*
*   **Breakdown**  (n): A failure of a vehicle or machine that stops it from working *(Sự hỏng hóc, chết máy)*
*   **Flat tire**  (n): A tire that has lost its air, usually because of a hole *(Lốp xe bị xẹp)*
*   **Fuel**  (n): A substance such as petrol or gas that is burned to produce power *(Nhiên liệu)*
*   **Odometer**  (n): An instrument in a vehicle that shows the total distance it has traveled *(Đồng hồ đo quãng đường)*
*   **Detour**  (n): A longer route taken to avoid a problem or to visit a place on the way *(Đường vòng)*
*   **Scenic**  (adj): Having or showing beautiful natural views *(Có phong cảnh đẹp)*
*   **Rest stop**  (n): A place beside a highway where drivers can stop to rest, eat, or use the toilet *(Trạm dừng chân)*
*   **Speeding**  (n): The act of driving faster than the legal limit *(Sự chạy quá tốc độ)*
*   **Tow**  (v): To pull a vehicle along behind another vehicle using a rope or chain *(Kéo (xe))*
*   **Dilemma**  (n): A situation in which you have to make a difficult choice between two options *(Tình thế khó xử, tiến thoái lưỡng nan)*
*   **Consequence**  (n): A result of an action, especially a bad or unpleasant one *(Hậu quả)*
*   **Hesitate**  (v): To pause before doing or saying something because you are uncertain or nervous *(Do dự, ngập ngừng)*
*   **Regret**  (v): To feel sorry or sad about something you did or did not do *(Hối tiếc)*
*   **Intuition**  (n): The ability to understand or know something immediately without conscious reasoning *(Trực giác)*
*   **Weigh up**  (phr v): To think carefully about the advantages and disadvantages of something before deciding *(Cân nhắc)*
*   **Rational**  (adj): Based on clear thought and reason rather than emotion *(Lý trí, hợp lý)*
*   **Trade-off**  (n): A balance between two things you cannot have at the same time, where gaining one means losing some of the other *(Sự đánh đổi)*
*   **Commit**  (v): To promise or decide firmly to do something or to give your time and energy to it *(Cam kết, toàn tâm)*
*   **Uncertainty**  (n): A situation in which something is not known or not sure *(Sự không chắc chắn, bất định)*
*   **Captivity**  (n): The state of being kept in a closed space and not allowed to go free *(Sự giam cầm, nuôi nhốt)*
*   **Enclosure**  (n): An area surrounded by a fence or wall, especially for keeping animals *(Khu vực rào kín, chuồng)*
*   **Poaching**  (n): The illegal hunting or catching of animals *(Nạn săn trộm)*
*   **Extinct**  (adj): (Of a type of animal or plant) no longer existing *(Tuyệt chủng)*
*   **Species**  (n): A group of animals or plants that share similar characteristics and can breed together *(Loài)*
*   **Sanctuary**  (n): A protected area where animals can live safely without being hunted *(Khu bảo tồn, nơi trú ẩn an toàn)*
*   **Migrate**  (v): (Of animals) to travel to a different place, usually when the season changes *(Di cư)*
*   **Prey**  (n): An animal that is hunted and eaten by another animal *(Con mồi)*
*   **Nocturnal**  (adj): (Of animals) active at night rather than during the day *(Hoạt động về đêm)*
*   **Release**  (v): To allow a person or animal to go free *(Thả, phóng thích)*
*   **Reservation**  (n): An arrangement to have a table, room, or seat kept for you *(Sự đặt chỗ trước)*
*   **Waiter**  (n): A person whose job is to serve food and drinks to customers in a restaurant *(Người phục vụ bàn)*
*   **Menu**  (n): A list of the food and drinks available in a restaurant *(Thực đơn)*
*   **Chef**  (n): A skilled professional cook, especially the head cook in a restaurant *(Đầu bếp)*
*   **Ingredient**  (n): One of the foods used to make a particular dish *(Nguyên liệu)*
*   **Appetizer**  (n): A small dish served before the main course of a meal *(Món khai vị)*
*   **Tip**  (n): An extra amount of money given to someone such as a waiter to thank them for their service *(Tiền boa)*
*   **Complaint**  (n): A statement saying that you are unhappy or unsatisfied about something *(Lời phàn nàn, khiếu nại)*
*   **Hygiene**  (n): The practice of keeping yourself and your surroundings clean to prevent disease *(Vệ sinh)*
*   **Overcooked**  (adj): Cooked for too long, so that it is dry, hard, or burnt *(Nấu quá chín, nấu quá lửa)*
*   **Ancestor**  (n): A family member from long ago, from whom you are descended *(Tổ tiên)*
*   **Firework**  (n): A small device that explodes to make loud noises and colorful lights in the sky *(Pháo hoa)*
*   **Lucky money**  (n): Money given in red envelopes during Lunar New Year to bring good luck *(Tiền lì xì, tiền mừng tuổi)*
*   **Reunion**  (n): An occasion when people who have been apart come together again *(Sự sum họp, đoàn tụ)*
*   **Tradition**  (n): A belief, custom, or way of doing something that has existed for a long time *(Truyền thống)*
*   **Superstition**  (n): A belief that certain events or actions bring good or bad luck, not based on reason *(Sự mê tín, điều kiêng kỵ)*
*   **Feast**  (n): A large, special meal, often to celebrate something *(Bữa tiệc, bữa cỗ)*
*   **Decorate**  (v): To make something look more attractive by adding things to it *(Trang trí)*
*   **Blessing**  (n): A wish for happiness and good fortune, or something that brings happiness *(Lời chúc phúc, phước lành)*
*   **Offering**  (n): Something given to a god or to the spirits of the dead as a sign of respect *(Lễ vật, đồ cúng)*
*   **Mindset**  (n): A person's way of thinking and their attitudes and opinions *(Tư duy, lối suy nghĩ)*
*   **Setback**  (n): A problem that delays or prevents progress *(Sự thất bại, trở ngại tạm thời)*
*   **Determination**  (n): The quality of continuing to try to do something even when it is difficult *(Sự quyết tâm)*
*   **Comfort zone**  (n): A situation in which you feel safe and comfortable and do not need to try anything difficult *(Vùng an toàn)*
*   **Potential**  (n): Qualities or abilities that can be developed and lead to future success *(Tiềm năng)*
*   **Overcome**  (v): To succeed in dealing with or controlling a problem or difficulty *(Vượt qua)*
*   **Self-improvement**  (n): The process of making yourself a better or more skilled person through your own efforts *(Sự tự hoàn thiện bản thân)*
*   **Progress**  (n): Movement toward reaching a goal or improving something *(Sự tiến bộ)*
*   **Achievement**  (n): Something successful that you have done, especially after a lot of effort *(Thành tựu)*
*   **Aspiration**  (n): A strong hope or wish to achieve something *(Khát vọng, nguyện vọng)*
