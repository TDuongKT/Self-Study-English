## 📝 English-English-Vietnamese Vocabulary List:

*   **Itinerary**  (n): A detailed plan or route of a journey *(Lịch trình chuyến đi, hành trình)*
*   **Authentic**  (adj): Real or true, not copied or fake *(Đích thực, chân thật, thật)*
*   **Heritage**  (n): Features belonging to the culture of a particular society, such as traditions, languages, or buildings, that were created in the past and still have historical importance *(Di sản)*
*   **Immerse**  (v): To involve yourself completely in an activity, environment, or situation *(Đắm chìm, hòa mình vào)*
*   **Excursion**  (n): A short journey usually made for pleasure, often by a group of people *(Chuyến tham quan, cuộc dã ngoại ngắn)*
*   **Magnificent**  (adj): Very good, beautiful, or deserving to be admired *(Tráng lệ, lộng lẫy, nguy nga)*
*   **Hospitality**  (n): The act of being friendly and welcoming to guests and visitors *(Lòng hiếu khách, sự mến khách)*
*   **Souvenir**  (n): Something you buy or keep to help you remember a holiday or special event *(Quà lưu niệm)*
*   **Exotic**  (adj): Unusual and exciting because of coming (or seeming to come) from far away *(Kỳ lạ, ngoại lai)*
*   **Wander**  (v): To walk around slowly in a relaxed way or without any clear purpose or direction *(Đi lang thang, thơ thẩn)*
*   **Investigate**  (v): To examine a crime, problem, statement, etc. carefully, especially to discover the truth *(Điều tra, nghiên cứu)*
*   **Suspect**  (n): A person believed to have committed a crime or done something wrong *(Nghi phạm, người bị tình nghi)*
*   **Evidence**  (n): One or more reasons for believing that something is or is not true, often used in court *(Bằng chứng, chứng cứ)*
*   **Confess**  (v): To admit that you have done something wrong or something that you feel guilty or bad about *(Thú nhận, thú tội)*
*   **Innocent**  (adj): (Of a person) not guilty of a particular crime *(Vô tội)*
*   **Verdict**  (n): An official decision made in a court of law *(Phán quyết, bản án)*
*   **Penalty**  (n): A punishment, or the usual punishment, for doing something that is against a law *(Hình phạt, án phạt)*
*   **Testimony**  (n): Spoken or written statements that something is true, especially those given in a law court *(Lời khai, lời chứng trước tòa)*
*   **Smuggle**  (v): To take things or people to or from a place secretly and often illegally *(Buôn lậu, lén lút mang vào)*
*   **Justice**  (n): Fairness in the way people are dealt with, or the system of laws that judges and punishes people *(Công lý, sự công bằng)*
*   **Terrain**  (n): An area of land, especially when considering its natural physical features *(Địa hình)*
*   **Summit**  (n): The highest point of a mountain or hill *(Đỉnh núi, hội nghị thượng đỉnh)*
*   **Abundance**  (n): A very large quantity or plentiful supply of something *(Sự phong phú, dồi dào, dạt dào)*
*   **Conservation**  (n): The protection and careful management of the environment and natural resources *(Sự bảo tồn)*
*   **Predator**  (n): An animal that naturally hunts and feeds on other animals *(Động vật săn mồi, thú ăn thịt)*
*   **Widespread**  (adj): Existing or happening over a large area or among many people *(Diện rộng, lan rộng)*
*   **Disastrous**  (adj): Causing great harm, damage, or extreme suffering *(Thảm họa, tai hại, thảm khốc)*
*   **Cultivate**  (v): To prepare land and grow crops, or to develop and foster a quality or skill *(Canh tác, trồng trọt / Vun đắp, trau dồi)*
*   **Harmony**  (n): A state of peaceful agreement, balance, and cooperation *(Sự hòa hợp, hòa thuận)*
*   **Erupt**  (v): To explode or burst out suddenly with force (especially of a volcano) *(Phun trào, bùng nổ)*
*   **Acquisition**  (n): The process of getting, learning, or developing something (such as knowledge or skills) *(Sự tiếp thu, tích lũy, đạt được)*
*   **Scholarship**  (n): Financial aid granted to a student to support their education *(Học bổng)*
*   **Privilege**  (n): A special advantage, right, or benefit granted to a particular person or group *(Đặc ân, đặc quyền)*
*   **Facilitate**  (v): To make a process, action, or task easier or smoother *(Tạo điều kiện, làm cho dễ dàng hơn)*
*   **Literacy**  (n): The ability to read, write, and process information effectively *(Năng lực biết đọc biết viết, biết chữ)*
*   **Stimulate**  (v): To encourage interest, activity, or intellectual development in something *(Kích thích, thúc đẩy)*
*   **Abstract**  (adj): Existing as an idea, feeling, or quality, rather than a concrete material object *(Trừu tượng)*
*   **Guidance**  (n): Help, advice, or direction given by an experienced or knowledgeable person *(Sự hướng dẫn, chỉ bảo)*
*   **Undertake**  (v): To commit oneself to and begin a task, project, or responsibility *(Đảm nhận, gánh vác, thực hiện)*
*   **Benchmark**  (n): A standard or point of reference against which things may be measured or evaluated *(Chuẩn mực, điểm mốc so sánh)*
