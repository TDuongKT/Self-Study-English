## 📝 English-English-Vietnamese Vocabulary List:

*   **Surveillance**  (n): Close observation of a person or group, especially one under suspicion *(Giám sát)*
*   **Encrypt**  (v): To convert information or data into a code, especially to prevent unauthorized access *(Mã hóa)*
*   **Breach**  (n): An act of breaking or failing to observe a law, agreement, or security measure *(Sự vi phạm, lỗ hổng bảo mật)*
*   **Anonymous**  (adj): Not identified by name; of unknown identity *(Nặc danh, ẩn danh)*
*   **Vulnerable**  (adj): Susceptible to being physically or emotionally harmed or attacked *(Dễ bị tổn thương)*
*   **Algorithm**  (n): A set of rules or steps followed, especially by a computer, to solve a problem *(Thuật toán)*
*   **Unprecedented**  (adj): Never having happened or existed before *(Chưa từng có tiền lệ)*
*   **Leverage**  (v): To use something to maximum advantage *(Tận dụng, sử dụng làm đòn bẩy)*
*   **Transparent**  (adj): Open and honest; not secretive about intentions or actions *(Minh bạch)*
*   **Safeguard**  (v): To protect someone or something from harm or damage *(Bảo vệ, bảo đảm an toàn)*
*   **Culinary**  (adj): Connected with cooking or the kitchen *(Thuộc về ẩm thực, nấu ăn)*
*   **Garnish**  (v): To decorate a dish of food with a small amount of another food *(Trang trí món ăn)*
*   **Palate**  (n): A person's ability to distinguish and appreciate different tastes; also the roof of the mouth *(Vị giác, khẩu vị)*
*   **Savor**  (v): To taste and enjoy food or drink completely *(Thưởng thức trọn vẹn)*
*   **Wholesome**  (adj): Good for physical, mental, or moral health *(Lành mạnh, bổ dưỡng)*
*   **Ferment**  (v): To undergo a chemical change caused by bacteria or yeast, usually producing bubbles or alcohol *(Lên men)*
*   **Aroma**  (n): A distinctive, pleasant smell, especially of food or drink *(Hương thơm)*
*   **Indulge**  (v): To allow oneself to enjoy the pleasure of something considered a treat *(Tự thưởng, nuông chiều bản thân)*
*   **Staple**  (n): A main or important food, item, or feature *(Thực phẩm/mặt hàng chủ lực)*
*   **Delicacy**  (n): A rare or expensive food considered especially pleasant to eat *(Món đặc sản, món ngon quý hiếm)*
*   **Underdog**  (n): A competitor thought to have little chance of winning *(Kẻ yếu thế, kẻ không được kỳ vọng)*
*   **Rigorous**  (adj): Extremely thorough, exhaustive, or accurate *(Nghiêm ngặt, khắt khe)*
*   **Endurance**  (n): The ability to withstand an unpleasant or difficult process without giving way; stamina *(Sức bền, sự chịu đựng)*
*   **Grueling**  (adj): Extremely tiring and demanding *(Gian khổ, kiệt sức)*
*   **Persevere**  (v): To continue in a course of action despite difficulty *(Kiên trì, bền chí)*
*   **Milestone**  (n): An action or event marking a significant stage in development *(Cột mốc quan trọng)*
*   **Momentum**  (n): The impetus and driving force gained by a developing process *(Đà, động lực)*
*   **Triumph**  (n): A great victory or achievement *(Chiến thắng vẻ vang)*
*   **Sportsmanship**  (n): Fair and generous behavior toward others, especially in a contest *(Tinh thần thể thao)*
*   **Withstand**  (v): To remain undamaged or unaffected by something; resist *(Chịu đựng, chống chịu)*
*   **Turbulent**  (adj): Characterized by conflict, disorder, or confusion; not stable or calm *(Hỗn loạn, náo động)*
*   **Torrential**  (adj): (Of rain) falling rapidly and in great quantity *(Như trút nước, xối xả)*
*   **Forecast**  (n): A prediction or estimate of future events, especially the weather *(Dự báo)*
*   **Erratic**  (adj): Not even or regular in pattern or movement; unpredictable *(Thất thường, khó lường)*
*   **Intensify**  (v): To become or make more intense *(Gia tăng, tăng cường)*
*   **Prolong**  (v): To extend the duration of something; make it last longer *(Kéo dài)*
*   **Devastate**  (v): To destroy or ruin something completely *(Tàn phá, hủy hoại)*
*   **Volatile**  (adj): Liable to change rapidly and unpredictably, especially for the worse *(Dễ biến động, thất thường)*
*   **Mitigate**  (v): To make something less severe, serious, or painful *(Giảm nhẹ, làm dịu)*
*   **Resilient**  (adj): Able to withstand or recover quickly from difficult conditions *(Kiên cường, có khả năng phục hồi)*
