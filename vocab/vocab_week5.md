## 📝 English-English-Vietnamese Vocabulary List:

*   **Reconcile**  (v): To restore friendly relations between people after a disagreement *(Hòa giải)*
*   **Empathy**  (n): The ability to understand and share the feelings of another *(Sự đồng cảm)*
*   **Candid**  (adj): Truthful and straightforward; frank *(Thẳng thắn, chân thật)*
*   **Resentment**  (n): Bitter indignation at having been treated unfairly *(Sự oán giận, bất mãn)*
*   **Reassure**  (v): To say or do something to remove someone's doubts or fears *(Trấn an, làm yên lòng)*
*   **Mutual**  (adj): Experienced or done by two or more parties toward each other; shared *(Lẫn nhau, tương hỗ)*
*   **Confide**  (v): To tell someone about a private matter while trusting them not to repeat it *(Tâm sự, thổ lộ)*
*   **Estrange**  (v): To cause someone to no longer feel close or friendly; alienate *(Làm xa cách, ly gián)*
*   **Compromise**  (n): An agreement reached by each side making concessions *(Sự thỏa hiệp)*
*   **Sincerity**  (n): The quality of being free from pretense or deceit; genuineness *(Sự chân thành)*
*   **Budget**  (n): An estimate of income and expenditure for a set period of time *(Ngân sách)*
*   **Frugal**  (adj): Sparing or economical with money or food *(Tiết kiệm, dè sẻn)*
*   **Deficit**  (n): The amount by which spending exceeds income; a shortfall *(Thâm hụt)*
*   **Invest**  (v): To put money into a financial scheme with the expectation of profit *(Đầu tư)*
*   **Debt**  (n): Money that is owed or due *(Nợ)*
*   **Prosperity**  (n): The state of being successful, especially financially; flourishing *(Sự thịnh vượng)*
*   **Squander**  (v): To waste money, time, or opportunities in a careless way *(Phung phí, tiêu xài hoang phí)*
*   **Inflation**  (n): A general increase in prices and fall in the purchasing value of money *(Lạm phát)*
*   **Withdraw**  (v): To take money out of a bank account *(Rút tiền)*
*   **Surplus**  (n): An amount left over when requirements have been met; excess *(Thặng dư, dư thừa)*
*   **Breakthrough**  (n): A sudden, dramatic, and important discovery or development *(Bước đột phá)*
*   **Celestial**  (adj): Relating to the sky or outer space *(Thuộc về bầu trời, thiên thể)*
*   **Orbit**  (n): The curved path of an object around a star, planet, or moon *(Quỹ đạo)*
*   **Vast**  (adj): Extremely great in size, area, or extent; immense *(Rộng lớn, bao la)*
*   **Hypothesis**  (n): A proposed explanation made on the basis of limited evidence, as a starting point for investigation *(Giả thuyết)*
*   **Phenomenon**  (n): A fact or situation observed to exist or happen, especially one whose cause is uncertain *(Hiện tượng)*
*   **Speculate**  (v): To form a theory about something without firm evidence *(Suy đoán, phỏng đoán)*
*   **Precision**  (n): The quality or fact of being exact and accurate *(Độ chính xác)*
*   **Unravel**  (v): To investigate and solve or explain something complicated *(Làm sáng tỏ, gỡ rối)*
*   **Infinite**  (adj): Limitless or endless in space, extent, or size *(Vô hạn)*
*   **Congestion**  (n): Overcrowding or blockage, especially of traffic *(Sự tắc nghẽn)*
*   **Commute**  (v): To travel some distance between one's home and place of work regularly *(Di chuyển đi làm hàng ngày)*
*   **Infrastructure**  (n): The basic physical and organizational structures needed for a society to operate *(Cơ sở hạ tầng)*
*   **Pedestrian**  (n): A person walking rather than traveling in a vehicle *(Người đi bộ)*
*   **Sprawl**  (n): The spreading of urban developments over undeveloped land near a city *(Sự lan rộng đô thị)*
*   **Gridlock**  (n): A situation in which traffic cannot move at all because roads are too crowded *(Tắc nghẽn giao thông hoàn toàn)*
*   **Skyline**  (n): An outline of land and buildings defined against the sky *(Đường chân trời thành phố)*
*   **Municipal**  (adj): Relating to a town or city, or its governing body *(Thuộc về thành phố, thị chính)*
*   **Accessible**  (adj): Able to be reached or entered; easy to obtain or use *(Dễ tiếp cận)*
*   **Revitalize**  (v): To give new life or energy to something *(Hồi sinh, làm sống động lại)*
