## 📝 English-English-Vietnamese Vocabulary List:

*   **Feed**  (n): A continuously updated stream of content on a social media platform *(Dòng tin, nguồn cấp dữ liệu)*
*   **Addictive**  (adj): Causing a strong desire to keep doing or using something; hard to stop *(Gây nghiện)*
*   **Notification**  (n): A message that appears to inform you about something, especially on a phone or app *(Thông báo)*
*   **Viral**  (adj): Spreading very quickly among many people, especially through the internet *(Lan truyền nhanh chóng)*
*   **Influencer**  (n): Someone who affects other people's opinions or buying habits through social media *(Người có ảnh hưởng)*
*   **Bandwidth**  (n): The amount of data that can be transmitted; figuratively, the mental capacity or time available *(Sức lực/khả năng xử lý, băng thông)*
*   **Glitch**  (n): A small, temporary problem or fault, especially in a machine or computer system *(Trục trặc nhỏ, sự cố kỹ thuật)*
*   **Overshare**  (v): To reveal too much personal information, especially online *(Chia sẻ quá mức)*
*   **Backlash**  (n): A strong negative reaction by a large number of people *(Làn sóng phản đối, phản ứng dữ dội)*
*   **Disconnect**  (v): To stop being connected; to detach from technology or from other people *(Ngắt kết nối)*
*   **Overwhelm**  (v): To have such a strong effect on someone that they don't know how to react *(Áp đảo, khiến choáng ngợp)*
*   **Burnout**  (n): Extreme tiredness or exhaustion caused by prolonged stress, especially from work *(Kiệt sức)*
*   **Cope**  (v): To deal successfully with a difficult situation *(Đối phó, ứng phó)*
*   **Anxious**  (adj): Feeling worried or nervous about something *(Lo lắng, bồn chồn)*
*   **Mindfulness**  (n): The practice of being fully aware and focused on the present moment *(Chánh niệm)*
*   **Vent**  (v): To express strong feelings, especially anger or frustration, openly *(Trút giận, giải tỏa cảm xúc)*
*   **Numb**  (adj): Unable to feel emotion; without physical sensation *(Tê liệt cảm xúc, mất cảm giác)*
*   **Solitude**  (n): The state of being alone, often in a peaceful or restful way *(Sự tĩnh lặng một mình, sự cô đơn)*
*   **Reflect**  (v): To think deeply or carefully about something *(Suy ngẫm, phản ánh)*
*   **Fulfilling**  (adj): Making someone feel satisfied and happy because it seems worthwhile *(Mang lại ý nghĩa, thỏa mãn)*
*   **Automate**  (v): To make a process operate automatically, using machines or computers instead of people *(Tự động hóa)*
*   **Autonomous**  (adj): Able to operate independently, without human control *(Tự chủ, tự động)*
*   **Simulate**  (v): To imitate the appearance, behavior, or conditions of something else, often using a computer *(Mô phỏng)*
*   **Displace**  (v): To take the place of someone or something; to force someone out of a job or position *(Thay thế, khiến mất việc)*
*   **Streamline**  (v): To make a process or system simpler and more efficient *(Hợp lý hóa, tinh giản)*
*   **Malfunction**  (n): A failure to function normally, especially of a machine or piece of equipment *(Trục trặc, hỏng hóc)*
*   **Sophisticated**  (adj): Highly developed, complex, and refined *(Tinh vi, tinh xảo, phức tạp)*
*   **Bias**  (n): An inclination or prejudice for or against something, especially in an unfair way *(Sự thiên vị, định kiến)*
*   **Ethical**  (adj): Relating to moral principles; concerned with what is morally right or wrong *(Thuộc về đạo đức)*
*   **Unemployment**  (n): The state of being without a paid job even though able to work *(Tình trạng thất nghiệp)*
*   **Nurture**  (v): To care for and encourage the growth or development of someone *(Nuôi dưỡng, chăm sóc)*
*   **Discipline**  (n): The practice of training people to obey rules, or the ability to control one's own behavior *(Kỷ luật)*
*   **Sibling**  (n): A brother or sister *(Anh chị em ruột)*
*   **Toddler**  (n): A young child who has only recently learned to walk *(Trẻ mới biết đi)*
*   **Upbringing**  (n): The way in which a child is cared for and taught how to behave while growing up *(Cách nuôi dạy, sự giáo dục con cái)*
*   **Overprotective**  (adj): Excessively protective of someone, often restricting their freedom unnecessarily *(Bảo bọc quá mức)*
*   **Household**  (n): A house and the people who live in it, regarded as a unit *(Hộ gia đình)*
*   **Chore**  (n): A routine task, especially a household one *(Việc vặt, việc nhà)*
*   **Bond**  (n): A close connection joining two or more people together *(Mối liên kết, sự gắn kết)*
*   **Role model**  (n): A person looked to by others as an example to imitate *(Hình mẫu, tấm gương)*
*   **Résumé**  (n): A short document describing a person's education, skills, and work experience, used when applying for jobs *(Sơ yếu lý lịch, hồ sơ xin việc)*
*   **Candidate**  (n): A person who applies for a job or is considered for a position *(Ứng viên)*
*   **Qualification**  (n): A skill, quality, or type of experience that makes someone suitable for a job *(Bằng cấp, trình độ chuyên môn)*
*   **Internship**  (n): A period of work experience offered by an organization, often to students or graduates *(Kỳ thực tập)*
*   **Networking**  (n): The activity of meeting and interacting with people to exchange information and develop useful contacts *(Xây dựng mạng lưới quan hệ)*
*   **Onboarding**  (n): The process of introducing and integrating a new employee into an organization *(Quá trình hòa nhập/đào tạo nhân viên mới)*
*   **Feedback**  (n): Information or opinions about someone's performance, used as a basis for improvement *(Phản hồi, góp ý)*
*   **Confidence**  (n): A feeling of self-assurance arising from an appreciation of one's own abilities *(Sự tự tin)*
*   **Persuasive**  (adj): Good at making people believe or do something through reasoning or argument *(Có sức thuyết phục)*
*   **Ambition**  (n): A strong desire to achieve something, typically requiring hard work and determination *(Tham vọng, hoài bão)*
*   **Insomnia**  (n): The habitual inability to sleep *(Chứng mất ngủ)*
*   **Drowsy**  (adj): Sleepy; feeling as if you want to fall asleep *(Buồn ngủ, lờ đờ)*
*   **Nap**  (n): A short sleep, especially one taken during the day *(Giấc ngủ ngắn, chợp mắt)*
*   **Routine**  (n): A sequence of actions regularly followed; a fixed schedule *(Thói quen, lịch trình hằng ngày)*
*   **Habitual**  (adj): Done constantly or repeatedly as a habit *(Theo thói quen, thường xuyên)*
*   **Sedentary**  (adj): Tending to spend a lot of time seated; involving little physical exercise *(Ít vận động, tĩnh tại)*
*   **Recharge**  (v): To rest or relax in order to restore one's energy *(Nạp lại năng lượng, hồi phục sức lực)*
*   **Deprive**  (v): To prevent someone from having or enjoying something, such as sleep *(Tước đoạt, thiếu (ngủ))*
*   **Alert**  (adj): Quick to notice and respond to things; attentive and awake *(Tỉnh táo, cảnh giác)*
*   **Consistency**  (n): The quality of always behaving or performing in the same way; steadiness *(Tính nhất quán, đều đặn)*
