## 📝 English-English-Vietnamese Vocabulary List:

*   **Bargain**  (n): Something bought or offered for sale at a price much lower than usual *(Món hời, món hàng giá rẻ)*
*   **Impulsive**  (adj): Acting suddenly without thinking carefully about what might happen *(Bốc đồng, hấp tấp)*
*   **Splurge**  (v): To spend a lot of money on something, especially something you don't really need *(Vung tiền, tiêu xài hoang phí)*
*   **Counterfeit**  (adj): Made to look exactly like something genuine in order to deceive people *(Giả, hàng nhái)*
*   **Refund**  (n): An amount of money that is given back to you, especially because you are not satisfied with a product *(Sự hoàn tiền)*
*   **Warranty**  (n): A written promise from a company to repair or replace a product if it breaks within a certain time *(Bảo hành, giấy bảo hành)*
*   **Retailer**  (n): A person or business that sells goods directly to the public *(Nhà bán lẻ)*
*   **Merchandise**  (n): Goods that are bought and sold *(Hàng hóa)*
*   **Discount**  (n): A reduction in the usual price of something *(Sự giảm giá, chiết khấu)*
*   **Materialistic**  (adj): Caring too much about money and possessions rather than more important things *(Coi trọng vật chất, thực dụng)*
*   **Legislation**  (n): A law or a set of laws made by a government *(Luật pháp, đạo luật)*
*   **Campaign**  (n): A planned series of activities designed to achieve a social, political, or commercial goal *(Chiến dịch, cuộc vận động)*
*   **Policy**  (n): A set of ideas or a plan of action officially agreed by a government, organization, or business *(Chính sách)*
*   **Referendum**  (n): A vote in which all the people of an area are asked to decide on a single political question *(Cuộc trưng cầu dân ý)*
*   **Ballot**  (n): A piece of paper used to vote secretly, or the process of voting *(Lá phiếu, cuộc bỏ phiếu)*
*   **Turnout**  (n): The number of people who vote in an election or attend an event *(Số người đi bầu, số người tham dự)*
*   **Corruption**  (n): Dishonest or illegal behavior, especially by people in power, such as accepting money in return for favors *(Tham nhũng)*
*   **Reform**  (n): A change made to a system or organization in order to improve it *(Sự cải cách)*
*   **Abolish**  (v): To officially end a law, system, or practice *(Bãi bỏ, hủy bỏ)*
*   **Democracy**  (n): A system of government in which people choose their leaders by voting *(Nền dân chủ)*
*   **Cramped**  (adj): Not having enough space for the people or things in it *(Chật chội, tù túng)*
*   **Landlord**  (n): A person who owns a building or apartment and rents it to other people *(Chủ nhà (cho thuê))*
*   **Tenant**  (n): A person who pays rent to live in or use a building or land owned by someone else *(Người thuê nhà)*
*   **Lease**  (n): A legal agreement in which someone pays to use a property for a fixed period of time *(Hợp đồng thuê)*
*   **Evict**  (v): To force someone to leave the place where they live, usually by law *(Đuổi ra khỏi nhà, trục xuất)*
*   **Relocate**  (v): To move to a new place to live or work *(Chuyển chỗ ở, di dời)*
*   **Spacious**  (adj): Having a lot of space; large and roomy *(Rộng rãi)*
*   **Mortgage**  (n): A loan from a bank used to buy a house, paid back over many years *(Khoản vay thế chấp mua nhà)*
*   **Renovate**  (v): To repair and improve an old building so that it is in good condition again *(Cải tạo, sửa sang)*
*   **Utilities**  (n): Basic services such as electricity, water, and gas that are supplied to a home *(Các dịch vụ tiện ích (điện, nước, ga))*
*   **Self-conscious**  (adj): Nervous or uncomfortable because you are worried about what other people think of you *(Ngại ngùng, mặc cảm, e dè)*
*   **Trendy**  (adj): Very fashionable and modern *(Hợp mốt, sành điệu)*
*   **Wardrobe**  (n): All the clothes that a person owns; also a tall cupboard for hanging clothes *(Tủ quần áo, toàn bộ quần áo)*
*   **Tailor**  (n): A person whose job is to make, repair, or adjust clothes *(Thợ may)*
*   **Fabric**  (n): Cloth or material used for making clothes, curtains, etc *(Vải, chất liệu vải)*
*   **Outfit**  (n): A set of clothes worn together, especially for a particular occasion *(Bộ trang phục)*
*   **Flattering**  (adj): Making someone look more attractive than usual *(Tôn dáng, làm đẹp hơn)*
*   **Accessory**  (n): An extra item such as a bag, belt, or jewelry that is worn with clothes *(Phụ kiện)*
*   **Outdated**  (adj): No longer fashionable or useful; old-fashioned *(Lỗi thời, lạc hậu)*
*   **Flaunt**  (v): To show something you own in an obvious way so that people will admire you *(Khoe khoang, phô trương)*
*   **Outbreak**  (n): The sudden start of something unpleasant, especially a disease *(Sự bùng phát (dịch bệnh))*
*   **Ward**  (n): A large room in a hospital where patients with similar conditions are treated *(Khu, phòng bệnh)*
*   **Paramedic**  (n): A person trained to give emergency medical care, usually in an ambulance *(Nhân viên cấp cứu y tế)*
*   **Contagious**  (adj): (Of a disease) able to be passed from one person to another by contact *(Dễ lây lan, truyền nhiễm)*
*   **Quarantine**  (n): A period of time when a person or animal is kept away from others to prevent a disease from spreading *(Sự cách ly)*
*   **Vaccine**  (n): A substance put into the body to protect it against a particular disease *(Vắc-xin)*
*   **Surgeon**  (n): A doctor who is trained to perform medical operations *(Bác sĩ phẫu thuật)*
*   **Dose**  (n): A measured amount of a medicine taken at one time *(Liều lượng, liều thuốc)*
*   **Side effect**  (n): An unpleasant extra effect that a medicine has on your body *(Tác dụng phụ)*
*   **Relapse**  (n): Becoming ill again after a period of improvement *(Sự tái phát)*
*   **Volunteer**  (n): A person who offers to do work without being paid *(Tình nguyện viên)*
*   **Nonprofit**  (adj): (Of an organization) not intended to make money, but to help people or support a cause *(Phi lợi nhuận)*
*   **Grassroots**  (adj): Started by ordinary people in a community rather than by leaders or officials *(Cơ sở, xuất phát từ người dân)*
*   **Donate**  (v): To give money, food, or goods to help a person or organization *(Quyên góp, hiến tặng)*
*   **Fundraiser**  (n): An event or activity organized to collect money for a particular purpose *(Sự kiện gây quỹ)*
*   **Generosity**  (n): The quality of being kind and willing to give money, help, or time *(Sự hào phóng, lòng rộng lượng)*
*   **Shelter**  (n): A place that gives temporary protection or housing to people in need *(Nơi trú ẩn, nhà tạm lánh)*
*   **Underprivileged**  (adj): Having less money and fewer opportunities than most people in society *(Thiệt thòi, kém may mắn)*
*   **Altruistic**  (adj): Showing concern for the happiness of other people rather than yourself *(Vị tha)*
*   **Compassion**  (n): A strong feeling of sympathy and desire to help people who are suffering *(Lòng trắc ẩn, lòng thương cảm)*
