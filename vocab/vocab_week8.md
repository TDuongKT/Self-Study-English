## 📝 English-English-Vietnamese Vocabulary List:

*   **Audition**  (n): A short performance given by an actor, musician, or dancer to show that they are suitable for a role *(Buổi thử giọng, buổi thử vai)*
*   **Stage fright**  (n): Nervousness felt before or during a performance in front of an audience *(Chứng sợ sân khấu)*
*   **Rehearsal**  (n): A time when all the people involved in a performance practice it before it is shown to the public *(Buổi tập dượt, diễn tập)*
*   **Melody**  (n): A series of musical notes that form a pleasant, recognizable tune *(Giai điệu)*
*   **Lyrics**  (n): The words of a song *(Lời bài hát)*
*   **Debut**  (n): The first public appearance of a performer or the first time something is shown to the public *(Màn ra mắt, buổi ra mắt)*
*   **Improvise**  (v): To perform or do something without preparation, inventing it as you go *(Ứng biến, ứng tác)*
*   **Captivate**  (v): To hold someone's attention completely by being extremely interesting or attractive *(Thu hút, làm say mê)*
*   **Applause**  (n): The sound of people clapping their hands to show approval or enjoyment *(Tràng pháo tay)*
*   **Encore**  (n): An extra performance given at the end of a show because the audience asks for it *(Màn biểu diễn thêm (theo yêu cầu khán giả))*
*   **Rural**  (adj): Relating to the countryside rather than towns and cities *(Thuộc về nông thôn)*
*   **Fertile**  (adj): (Of land) able to produce a lot of healthy crops and plants *(Màu mỡ, phì nhiêu)*
*   **Pesticide**  (n): A chemical used to kill insects or small animals that damage crops *(Thuốc trừ sâu)*
*   **Harvest**  (n): The time or activity of gathering crops; the amount of crops collected *(Vụ thu hoạch, mùa gặt)*
*   **Barren**  (adj): (Of land) too poor to produce crops or plants *(Cằn cỗi, khô cằn)*
*   **Organic**  (adj): Grown or produced without using artificial chemicals *(Hữu cơ)*
*   **Irrigation**  (n): The supply of water to land or crops through pipes or channels *(Sự tưới tiêu, thủy lợi)*
*   **Livestock**  (n): Animals such as cows, pigs, and sheep that are kept on a farm *(Gia súc, vật nuôi)*
*   **Crop**  (n): A plant such as a grain, fruit, or vegetable grown in large amounts, or the amount gathered in one season *(Cây trồng, mùa vụ)*
*   **Yield**  (n): The amount of something, such as crops, that is produced *(Sản lượng, năng suất)*
*   **Excavate**  (v): To dig in the ground to look for old buried objects in order to learn about the past *(Khai quật)*
*   **Ruins**  (n): The parts of a building or town that remain after it has been destroyed or has fallen down *(Tàn tích, phế tích)*
*   **Ancient**  (adj): From a very long time ago; very old *(Cổ đại, cổ xưa)*
*   **Civilization**  (n): A human society with its own highly developed social organization, culture, and way of life *(Nền văn minh)*
*   **Artifact**  (n): An object made by a person, especially one that is historically or culturally interesting *(Hiện vật, đồ tạo tác)*
*   **Inscription**  (n): Words that are written or carved on something, such as stone or metal *(Dòng chữ khắc, văn khắc)*
*   **Decipher**  (v): To discover the meaning of something that is difficult to read or understand *(Giải mã)*
*   **Archive**  (n): A collection of historical documents or records, or the place where they are kept *(Kho lưu trữ, văn khố)*
*   **Dynasty**  (n): A series of rulers of a country who all belong to the same family *(Triều đại)*
*   **Descendant**  (n): A person related to someone who lived a long time ago *(Hậu duệ, con cháu)*
*   **Engagement**  (n): An agreement to get married to someone, or the period of time before the wedding *(Sự đính hôn, lễ ăn hỏi)*
*   **Ceremony**  (n): A formal public event, often with traditional actions, held to celebrate something important *(Nghi lễ, buổi lễ)*
*   **Vow**  (n): A serious promise, especially one made during a wedding *(Lời thề, lời nguyện ước)*
*   **Reception**  (n): A formal party held to welcome someone or to celebrate an event such as a wedding *(Tiệc chiêu đãi, tiệc cưới)*
*   **Festive**  (adj): Relating to a celebration; cheerful and joyful *(Mang không khí lễ hội, vui tươi)*
*   **Banquet**  (n): A large formal meal for many people, often to celebrate a special occasion *(Bữa tiệc lớn, yến tiệc)*
*   **Toast**  (n): The act of raising glasses and drinking to wish someone happiness or success *(Lời chúc rượu, nâng ly chúc mừng)*
*   **Newlywed**  (n): A person who has recently married *(Người mới cưới, vợ chồng mới cưới)*
*   **Anniversary**  (n): The day on which an important event happened in a previous year *(Ngày kỷ niệm)*
*   **Commemorate**  (v): To do something special to remember and show respect for an important event or person *(Kỷ niệm, tưởng niệm)*
*   **Coral reef**  (n): A long line of hard coral that forms underwater near the surface of warm seas *(Rạn san hô)*
*   **Marine**  (adj): Relating to the sea or the animals and plants that live in it *(Thuộc về biển, hải dương)*
*   **Biodiversity**  (n): The variety of plants and animals living in a particular area *(Đa dạng sinh học)*
*   **Overfishing**  (n): Catching so many fish that there are not enough left to breed and maintain the population *(Đánh bắt quá mức)*
*   **Deplete**  (v): To reduce something, such as a supply or resource, by a large amount *(Làm cạn kiệt, suy giảm)*
*   **Pollutant**  (n): A substance that makes air, water, or land dirty and harmful *(Chất gây ô nhiễm)*
*   **Tide**  (n): The regular rise and fall of the sea level, which happens twice a day *(Thủy triều)*
*   **Shoreline**  (n): The edge of a sea, lake, or wide river *(Bờ biển, đường bờ)*
*   **Drift**  (v): To be carried slowly by the movement of water or air *(Trôi dạt)*
*   **Submerge**  (v): To put something under the surface of water, or to go below it *(Nhấn chìm, lặn xuống)*
*   **Micromanage**  (v): To control every small detail of other people's work, often in an annoying way *(Quản lý vi mô, can thiệp quá chi tiết)*
*   **Morale**  (n): The level of confidence and enthusiasm felt by a person or group *(Tinh thần, sĩ khí)*
*   **Delegate**  (v): To give part of your work, power, or responsibility to someone else *(Ủy quyền, giao việc)*
*   **Prioritize**  (v): To decide which tasks are most important and deal with them first *(Ưu tiên)*
*   **Accountable**  (adj): Responsible for your decisions or actions and expected to explain them *(Chịu trách nhiệm)*
*   **Hierarchy**  (n): A system in which people are organized into levels of importance from highest to lowest *(Hệ thống cấp bậc, thứ bậc)*
*   **Initiative**  (n): The ability to decide and act on your own without waiting for someone to tell you *(Sự chủ động, sáng kiến)*
*   **Consensus**  (n): General agreement among all the members of a group *(Sự đồng thuận)*
*   **Deadline**  (n): A time or date by which something must be finished *(Hạn chót)*
*   **Visionary**  (adj): Having original ideas about the future and the ability to plan for it *(Có tầm nhìn xa)*
