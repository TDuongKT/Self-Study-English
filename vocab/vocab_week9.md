## 📝 English-English-Vietnamese Vocabulary List:

*   **Departure**  (n): The act of leaving a place, especially at the start of a journey *(Sự khởi hành, chuyến đi)*
*   **Boarding pass**  (n): A card that a passenger must show before getting on a plane *(Thẻ lên máy bay)*
*   **Layover**  (n): A short stay in a place between two parts of a journey, especially between flights *(Thời gian quá cảnh, chặng dừng)*
*   **Delay**  (n): A period of time by which something is late or postponed *(Sự chậm trễ, sự trì hoãn)*
*   **Customs**  (n): The place at an airport or border where officials check bags for illegal goods and collect taxes *(Hải quan)*
*   **Luggage**  (n): The bags and suitcases that a person carries when traveling *(Hành lý)*
*   **Jet lag**  (n): A feeling of tiredness and confusion after a long flight across different time zones *(Mệt mỏi do lệch múi giờ)*
*   **Runway**  (n): A long, flat strip of ground where planes take off and land *(Đường băng)*
*   **Cabin crew**  (n): The people whose job is to take care of passengers on a plane *(Tiếp viên hàng không, phi hành đoàn)*
*   **Overbooked**  (adj): Having sold more tickets or reservations than there are seats or rooms available *(Bán vượt số chỗ, đặt chỗ quá tải)*
*   **Introvert**  (n): A quiet person who prefers to spend time alone or with a few people rather than in large groups *(Người hướng nội)*
*   **Extrovert**  (n): An energetic person who enjoys being with other people and feels comfortable in social situations *(Người hướng ngoại)*
*   **Temperament**  (n): A person's natural character and the way they usually behave or react *(Tính khí, khí chất)*
*   **Stubborn**  (adj): Refusing to change your opinion or behavior even when there are good reasons to *(Bướng bỉnh, cứng đầu)*
*   **Optimistic**  (adj): Hopeful and confident that good things will happen in the future *(Lạc quan)*
*   **Pessimistic**  (adj): Expecting bad things to happen or that something will not succeed *(Bi quan)*
*   **Perfectionist**  (n): A person who wants everything to be perfect and is unhappy with anything less *(Người cầu toàn)*
*   **Insecure**  (adj): Not confident about yourself or your abilities; feeling anxious and uncertain *(Thiếu tự tin, bất an)*
*   **Modest**  (adj): Not talking much about your own abilities or achievements *(Khiêm tốn)*
*   **Impatient**  (adj): Easily annoyed by delays; wanting something to happen as soon as possible *(Thiếu kiên nhẫn, nóng vội)*
*   **Headline**  (n): The title of a newspaper story, printed in large letters above it *(Tiêu đề, tít báo)*
*   **Editor**  (n): A person who is in charge of a newspaper or magazine, or who checks and corrects texts before they are published *(Biên tập viên, tổng biên tập)*
*   **Correspondent**  (n): A journalist who reports news from a particular place or on a particular subject *(Phóng viên thường trú, phóng viên chuyên mục)*
*   **Credible**  (adj): Able to be believed or trusted *(Đáng tin cậy)*
*   **Misinformation**  (n): False or incorrect information, especially when it is spread widely *(Thông tin sai lệch)*
*   **Exclusive**  (n): A news story that only one newspaper or channel has published *(Tin độc quyền)*
*   **Sensational**  (adj): Presenting news in a way that is intended to shock or excite people rather than inform them *(Giật gân)*
*   **Retract**  (v): To officially say that something you said or published earlier was wrong *(Rút lại (tuyên bố, bài báo), cải chính)*
*   **Censorship**  (n): The practice of controlling or removing parts of books, films, news, etc. that are considered unacceptable *(Sự kiểm duyệt)*
*   **Circulation**  (n): The number of copies of a newspaper or magazine that are sold or distributed regularly *(Số lượng phát hành)*
*   **Insurance**  (n): An agreement in which you pay a company regularly and they pay you money if something bad happens *(Bảo hiểm)*
*   **Premium**  (n): The amount of money that you pay regularly for insurance *(Phí bảo hiểm)*
*   **Claim**  (n): An official request for money that you believe you have a right to, especially from an insurance company *(Yêu cầu bồi thường)*
*   **Deductible**  (n): The amount of money you must pay yourself before an insurance company pays the rest *(Mức khấu trừ, khoản tự chi trả)*
*   **Interest rate**  (n): The percentage that a bank charges for lending money or pays for keeping money in an account *(Lãi suất)*
*   **Loan**  (n): An amount of money that is borrowed and must be paid back, usually with interest *(Khoản vay)*
*   **Collateral**  (n): Property or other valuable things that you promise to give a lender if you cannot pay back a loan *(Tài sản thế chấp)*
*   **Savings**  (n): Money that you have kept, especially in a bank, so that you can use it later *(Tiền tiết kiệm)*
*   **Transaction**  (n): An occasion when money is paid, received, or moved, for example in a bank *(Giao dịch)*
*   **Fraud**  (n): The crime of deceiving people in order to get money or goods illegally *(Sự gian lận, lừa đảo)*
*   **Blueprint**  (n): A detailed technical drawing or plan for a building or machine *(Bản thiết kế, bản vẽ kỹ thuật)*
*   **Facade**  (n): The front wall of a large building, especially one that faces a street *(Mặt tiền (tòa nhà))*
*   **Skyscraper**  (n): A very tall modern building in a city *(Tòa nhà chọc trời)*
*   **Landmark**  (n): A famous building or place that is easy to recognize and helps people know where they are *(Địa danh, công trình biểu tượng)*
*   **Aesthetic**  (adj): Relating to beauty and the appreciation of beauty *(Thuộc về thẩm mỹ)*
*   **Contractor**  (n): A person or company that is paid to do work, especially building work *(Nhà thầu)*
*   **Foundation**  (n): The solid structure under the ground that supports a building *(Nền móng)*
*   **Durable**  (adj): Able to last for a long time without being damaged *(Bền, lâu bền)*
*   **Symmetrical**  (adj): Having two halves that are exactly the same *(Cân xứng, đối xứng)*
*   **Ventilation**  (n): The system or process of allowing fresh air to enter and move around a room or building *(Sự thông gió)*
*   **Adopt**  (v): To take an animal or child into your home and care for it as your own *(Nhận nuôi)*
*   **Stray**  (adj): (Of an animal) lost or having no home *(Đi lạc, vô chủ)*
*   **Breed**  (n): A particular type of animal, especially one developed by people *(Giống (vật nuôi))*
*   **Veterinarian**  (n): A doctor who is trained to treat sick or injured animals *(Bác sĩ thú y)*
*   **Loyal**  (adj): Always supporting and staying faithful to a person or thing *(Trung thành)*
*   **Neglect**  (v): To fail to take care of someone or something properly *(Bỏ bê, sao nhãng)*
*   **Leash**  (n): A long piece of rope or leather fastened to a dog's collar to control it *(Dây dắt (chó))*
*   **Obedient**  (adj): Doing what you are told to do *(Vâng lời, ngoan ngoãn)*
*   **Groom**  (v): To clean and brush an animal's fur to keep it neat and healthy *(Chải chuốt, chăm sóc lông (thú cưng))*
*   **Companionship**  (n): The pleasant feeling of having a friend or companion with you *(Sự bầu bạn, đồng hành)*
